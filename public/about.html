🔒 For Parents: How This Site Works

THE SAFETY RULES

Nothing Is Collected

There are no accounts, no sign-ups, no comment boxes, no chat, and no forms that send anything anywhere. Games, makers, flashcards and checklists all run inside the visitor's own browser; scores and lists are stored only on that device. Nothing about any visitor is collected, shared, or sold.

ABOUT ADS

Child-Directed Mode Only

If ads appear on this site, they are configured for child-directed, non-personalized delivery — no ad tracking of kids, no personalized advertising. The music is by 3000 Studios (the family's own studio), and band content is fan talk in our own words — no lyrics, album art, or band recordings.
