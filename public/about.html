ABOUT THIS SITE

Games, makers, study tools and music — all in one place, all running right in your browser.

🔒 For Parents: How This Site Works

THE SAFETY RULES

Almost Nothing Is Stored

There are no public accounts, sign-ups, chat rooms or uploads. The only thing this site stores is the invite-only Friends Message Board: short text messages from friends a parent personally invited, shown under a nickname the parent chose. Links, emails, phone numbers and addresses are removed automatically, a parent can delete any message, and every message auto-deletes after 30 days. Games, makers, flashcards and checklists all run inside the visitor's own browser; scores and lists are stored only on that device. Nothing else about any visitor is collected, and nothing is ever shared or sold.

ABOUT ADS

Child-Directed Mode Only

If ads appear on this site, they are configured for child-directed, non-personalized delivery — no ad tracking of kids, no personalized advertising. There are no lyrics, album art, or recordings from other artists on this site.
