💛 Friends Zone

This whole site is built for the "grab a friend and sit side by side" style of hanging out. The fun is IRL: you make it, you download it, you show it off in person. And when the crew is apart, the Haunted Message Board below is the place to say hey.

🏠 The House Rules

1
Pass and play. One device, two players, take turns. Winners pick the next game. Losers get a rematch — always.
2
Make it, download it, show it. Creations save to the device, not to the internet. Trading designs happens face-to-face, like trading stickers. Because it basically is trading stickers.
3
Keep it kind. Hype each other's designs. Roast the games, never the person. If a deck design is ugly, the correct review is "bold choice" and a sticker swap.
4
Be safe out there. Never share your last name, address, school, phone number or email — not here, not anywhere online. The message board removes that stuff automatically, but the best filter is you.

🏆 The Challenges

Official crew competitions. Bragging rights are forever (or until the rematch).

  • Deck Battle — Both players design a skateboard deck in the Deck Designer in under 5 minutes. Download both. A third friend (or a parent, bribed with snacks) judges. Categories: Coolest, Funniest, Most Checkerboard.
  • Avatar Swap — Design an avatar of each other in the Avatar Maker. Accuracy is optional. Flattery is mandatory.
  • Beat Battle — Back-to-back runs of Beat Tap. Highest score picks the next song the whole room has to listen to. No veto power. Democracy is beautiful.
  • Highway Duel — Note Highway, alternating runs. Combined combo count is the tiebreaker. Air guitar between runs is scored by the audience (it's always a 10).
  • Memory Royalty — Memory Match with the fewest moves wins the crown. The crown is imaginary. The glory is real.
  • Poster Night — Both players make a band poster for the same imaginary concert. Print them, hang them, argue about whose is better until someone's mom picks a winner.
  • Skate Dash Showdown — Skate Dash tournament. Whoever holds the house record picks the next song on the player.
