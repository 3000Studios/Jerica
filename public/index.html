🎸 The Grunge & Metal Wall

Two bands that rewrote what loud could be. Fan talk in our own words — no lyrics, logos or band recordings here.

GRUNGE · ABERDEEN, WA · 1987

Nirvana

Three people, cheap amps and quiet-to-LOUD songs that dragged grunge out of Seattle basements and onto every radio in 1991. Proof that raw beats polished.

THRASH METAL · LOS ANGELES · 1981

Metallica

Lightning-fast riffs, thunder drums and the 1991 black album that turned heavy metal into a worldwide thing. Air guitar is mandatory.

HOUSE RULES

The Wall’s Code

Black band tee: required. Volume: up. Headbanging: strongly encouraged — just mind the furniture and the lamp.

🖤 The Emo & Alt Shelf

GOTH ROCK · ENGLAND · 1978

The Cure

Wild hair, smudged eyeliner and moody, shimmering guitars. One of the bands that started the whole goth look.

GOTHIC ROCK · ARKANSAS · 1995

Evanescence

Huge piano, heavy guitars and a voice that sounds like it is echoing through an empty cathedral.

EMO · NEW JERSEY · 2001

My Chemical Romance

Marching-band drama crashed into punk rock. Black outfits and giant choruses built for a haunted parade.

NU METAL · CALIFORNIA · 1996

Linkin Park

Rapping, screaming, turntables and electronics all smashed together into the sound of the early 2000s.

POP-PUNK · TENNESSEE · 2004

Paramore

Bright hair, fast drums and choruses made for yelling along with the car windows down.

PUNK · CALIFORNIA · 1987

Green Day

Three chords and zero chill — the band that proved punk can be loud and fun at the same time.
